# the bloat wave already reduced to its floor: a denied-verb enumeration, a
# multi-platform isolation contract, a multi-axis discovery rule), NOT a
# contamination hit widened in to make the gate pass. A new over-cap bullet
# must be EITHER reduced OR added here WITH a reason (a reviewed governance
# act); it is NEVER auto-allowlisted.
#

#            and the gate re-fires, forcing re-adjudication)
#   reason:  why this bullet is irreducibly over-cap (load-bearing)
