---
name: planning
description: Use when scoping non-trivial work, sequencing implementation, or deciding how to verify a change.
---

1. Restate the goal in repo-specific terms.
2. Identify the real files, modules, and boundaries involved.
3. Break the work into small ordered steps.
4. Name verification steps before editing.
5. Call out risks, unknowns, and rollback points.
6. Do not invent APIs or configuration behavior.
