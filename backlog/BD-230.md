The eventual fix-coder applies the user-chosen scope; the boundary-pure default is (a).
  **Solution:** per the user-chosen scope, remove/rewrite the `BD-NNN` references in `supporting-docs/` so no pack-internal tracking ID ships to clients; verify with a grep-zero (or grep-equals-allowlist) completeness gate.
